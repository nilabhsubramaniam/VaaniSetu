What is VaaniSetu?

Most voice assistants send your speech to a remote server, and most of them handle Hindi–English code-switching — completely ordinary speech for a huge number of people — poorly. VaaniSetu is voice-first, built around Hindi and Hinglish rather than translated into them, and designed so the components that hear, understand, and reply to you run on your own hardware by default.

Built for Indian languages, not just Hindi

Language is a shared, typed concept across the app rather than something assumed. Two languages are active in the current UI; the rest are already part of the plan, enabled one at a time as each meets its quality bar.

Local-first, by design

VaaniSetu is designed with local-first processing and user control over data as core principles: every component in the eventual voice loop — listening, understanding, retrieving, and replying — is meant to run on your own hardware by default, with a cloud model only ever an explicit, revocable choice, never a silent fallback.

Today, the assistant workspace makes no network calls and stores nothing beyond a language preference in your browser — there's simply nothing to process yet. The principles above describe what this project is building toward.

Why VaaniSetu

Capable open-source speech and language models for Indian languages already exist. Turning them into one coherent, private assistant that speaks the way people actually speak — Hindi, English, and the two mixed together — is still an unsolved integration problem. That's the gap VaaniSetu is built to close.
